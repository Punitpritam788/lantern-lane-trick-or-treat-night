🎃 Lantern Lane

Trick-or-Treat Night

Halloween is this week’s biggest trend. Tonight you’re a little lantern-witch exploring Old Hollow, an endless graveyard where restless ghosts guard the candy they never got to eat. Gather candy to wake the pumpkin boss, then defeat it with your lantern pulse.

Endings found: 0/3 · All art, models and sound are generated by code.

How to Play

The graveyard never ends. Gravestones, trees and pumpkins block you, but ghosts float right through them. Goal: collect 🍬 20 candies. Baron Gourdo wakes up. Hit him 9 times with your lantern pulse. Pulses also zap ghosts (+10).

Ghost touches cost a heart (you have 3). Keep all 3 for the Perfect ending.

When a ghost gets close, the camera switches to first person and faces the nearest threat, and movement becomes camera-relative until you’re clear (shown by 👁).

Nemesis: any ghost that hurts you is promoted into a named nemesis (up to Lv 5) with traits Swift, Armored or Sneaky. They come back, even in later runs. Pulse them several times to vanquish them for a big bonus and candy.

♾ Endless Siege: no candy goal. Survive wave after wave of ghosts; every wave clear heals a heart and upgrades your pulse, every 5th wave Baron Gourdo returns, and nemeses stalk you the whole time. Your best wave is saved.

⚡ Dash: Shift / Q, gamepad B, or the 💨 button: a quick burst with brief invulnerability. Level up: zaps, candy and wave clears give XP, and each level lets you pick an upgrade (wider pulse, aura, magnet, extra hearts…).

P / Esc pause · M mute · Skip any cutscene with Space, Enter, Esc or the button.
